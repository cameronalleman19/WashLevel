Sidecar Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

In plain English

Sidecar reads your Dencar reports in your own browser and keeps the results there. Nothing about your wash — sales, members, consumers, VIA exceptions — is ever uploaded to WashLevel. The only things our servers ever see are your license key and, if you subscribe, the billing details you hand directly to Stripe.

What Sidecar Is

WashLevel Sidecar ("Sidecar") is a browser extension built by WashLevel that reads a car wash operator's own data from Dencar (admin.dencar.sancsoft.net) and displays it as dashboards — sales summaries, retail performance, membership health, and VIA Guard exception review — inside your browser. This policy covers the Sidecar extension and the washlevel.com/sidecar pages used to purchase and manage it. It does not cover Dencar or Sanc Soft, which are operated independently — see their own privacy policy for how they handle your data.

Data That Stays On Your Device

When you click Sync, Sidecar reads report pages, VIA exceptions, consumer records, and payment history from Dencar using your own already-open, already-authenticated Dencar session — the same session your browser already has from logging in normally. The results are stored using chrome.storage.local, a storage area built into your browser and tied to your device. This can include:

  • Daily site reports, revenue figures, and wash counts
  • Consumer and member records visible in your own Dencar account, including names and vehicle/plate information
  • Payment history and membership billing events shown in Dencar's own reports
  • VIA Guard exceptions, your notes on them, and your past decisions

None of this is transmitted to WashLevel's servers. It never leaves your browser except when your browser itself talks to Dencar to fetch it. If you uninstall Sidecar or clear your browser's extension storage, this data is deleted with it.

How Sidecar Accesses Dencar

Sidecar requests browser permission to read pages on admin.dencar.sancsoft.net. This permission is used only to fetch the reports, exceptions, and records described above, using the Dencar session your browser already has open. Sidecar never asks for, reads, or stores your Dencar username or password — it rides on your existing login the same way any tab you already have open would.

Service Providers

WashLevel uses a small number of processors to run Sidecar's licensing and billing, each limited to what it needs to do its job:

  • Stripe — processes checkout, subscriptions, and the billing portal. Stripe handles your payment details directly; WashLevel never receives your full card number.
  • Resend — delivers the transactional email containing your license key after purchase.
  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — hosts the license validation, checkout, and webhook functions, and stores license and subscription records (email, license key, subscription status). It does not store any wash, consumer, or member data — that never reaches our servers in the first place.

None of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own purposes, and none of them receive your Dencar data, since that data never reaches WashLevel to begin with.

How We Use Data, and What We Don't Do

Data that does reach WashLevel (your license key, email, and subscription status) is used solely to operate Sidecar's licensing and billing — confirming an active subscription and sending you your key and receipts. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build ad-targeting profiles, we do not sell or rent it to third parties, and we do not run analytics or tracking scripts inside the extension. Sidecar's own service-worker code makes no outbound network requests beyond Dencar (to sync your data) and washlevel.com (to check your license and, if you ask, open the billing portal).

Data Retention & Deletion

Data stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local) stays until you uninstall Sidecar or clear it yourself. License and subscription records are kept for as long as your subscription is active plus a reasonable period for billing records, then may be deleted on request. To request deletion of your license/subscription record, or ask any question about your data, contact us using the details below.

Your Choices

  • Uninstall Sidecar at any time from chrome://extensions — this removes all locally stored wash data immediately.
  • Cancel your subscription any time from the Manage Billing button in Sidecar's Settings tab, or by calling us — cancelling during your trial means you're never charged.
  • Ask us to delete your license/subscription record by contacting support.

Children's Privacy

Sidecar is a business tool built for car wash operators and is not directed at, marketed to, or knowingly used by children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we materially change what data Sidecar collects or how we use it, we'll update this page, update the effective date above, and note the change inside the extension (such as in the Settings tab or an update notice) rather than only relying on this page being re-read.
