What you get

Five screens Dencar doesn't have

Overview

Every site, one page

Today, yesterday, week and month to date, plus a month-end projection that updates as the month fills in. Hourly wash counts show you when your tunnel actually earns.

Retail

Retail dollars per wash

Membership billing is stripped out — no pass renewals, no VIA charges — so the number you see is what retail customers actually paid. Day-of-week baselines flag the days that quietly underperform.

Members

Who's about to cancel

Members get a risk score from their own usage history: gone dormant, washing less than they used to, new signups who never came back. Cohort curves show how each month's signups are holding up.

VIA Guard

A call on every exception

Each exception arrives with a recommendation — trigger or let it slide — based on how much that member actually washes, whether the plate is a near match, and whether they've done it before. Trigger or close without leaving the page.

Consumers

Real washes per month

Usage counted from payment history and adjusted for how long someone's been a member, so a two-week-old signup isn't compared against a two-year veteran.

Anomalies

Days that don't look right

Sidecar compares each day against that site's own pattern and surfaces the outliers — before you find them in a month-end review.

No. Sidecar works with the access you already have. There's nothing to enable on Dencar's side and nothing to ask them for.

Where does my data go?

Into your own browser's storage, and nowhere else. Sidecar reads your Dencar reports the same way your browser does and keeps the results locally. WashLevel only ever sees your license key.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. The same key works on any browser you install it in. Each one keeps its own synced history.
