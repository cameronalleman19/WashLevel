Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need both Dencar and CryptoPay?

No. Sidecar works with either platform on its own or both together. If you only use Dencar, you get the Dencar features. If you only use CryptoPay, you get the CryptoPay features. If you use both, everything connects into one dashboard.

Do I need anything from Dencar or CryptoPay to use this?

No. Sidecar works with the access you already have. There's nothing to enable on either platform's side and nothing to ask them for.

Where does my data go?

Into your own browser's storage, and nowhere else. Sidecar reads your Dencar and CryptoPay data the same way your browser does and keeps the results locally. WashLevel only ever sees your license key.

What happens to my price in 2027?

Nothing — if you subscribe before the end of 2026, you keep $29/month for as long as your subscription stays active. The $39 price only applies to new subscribers starting in January.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome on Windows and Mac today, installed from the Chrome Web Store. A Safari version is in the works — your subscription covers both, so there's nothing extra to buy when it lands.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. The same key works on any browser you install it in. Each one keeps its own synced history.
